LinguaLink — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

LinguaLink ("the app", "we") is a real-time translation chat and calling application. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, and your chosen spoken/translation language.
  • Optional profile information: phone number (used only for future contact-matching features; not required).
  • Content you send: text messages, voice messages, images, and videos that you send in chats, for the sole purpose of translating and delivering them.
  • Usage information: number of messages sent and call minutes used, to enforce your subscription plan's limits.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your content is processed only to provide the app's core features:

  • Speech-to-text transcription, text translation, and text-to-speech generation.
  • Text extraction and translation from images (OCR).
  • Real-time voice and video call translation.
  • Storing and syncing your conversations across your devices.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide these features, relevant content is sent to the following processors, subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage.
  • OpenAI — speech transcription, translation, and image text recognition.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech voice generation.
  • Agora — real-time voice and video call infrastructure.
  • Google — optional "Sign in with Google" authentication.

We do not sell your personal information or message content to any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

Messages and media are retained until you delete them or delete your account. You may request full account and data deletion at any time by contacting us using the email below.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

LinguaLink is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
