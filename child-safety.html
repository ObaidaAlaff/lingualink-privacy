LinguaLink — Child Safety Standards

App: LinguaLink

LinguaLink has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) material or behavior of any kind. This page describes our standards and how to report a concern.

Our commitment

  • We prohibit the creation, upload, sharing, or storage of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) anywhere in LinguaLink, including in chat messages, voice notes, images, and videos.
  • Accounts found to violate this policy are suspended and, where legally required, reported to the relevant national authorities and to organizations such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations related to child safety.

How to report a concern

In the app: open your Profile → "Report a Concern" (under Support & Safety), which opens a pre-addressed email to our safety contact.

By email: oalaff@smail.ucas.edu.ps

Include as much detail as possible (account name/email involved, description of the content or behavior, and screenshots if available). We review every report and take action, including account suspension and legal reporting where applicable.

Contact for compliance and law enforcement

Our designated contact for child safety compliance matters is oalaff@smail.ucas.edu.ps.
