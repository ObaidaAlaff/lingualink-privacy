LinguaLink — Delete Your Account

App: LinguaLink · Developer: LinguaLink Team

You can request permanent deletion of your LinguaLink account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how.

How to request deletion

Send an email to oalaff@smail.ucas.edu.ps with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", sent from the email address registered to your LinguaLink account (or stating your registered email/account name in the message).

We reply to confirm receipt within a few days, and complete the deletion within 14 days of a verified request.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, phone number if provided, preferred language).
  • Your account credentials (you will no longer be able to sign in).
  • Messages, voice notes, images, and videos you sent, and their translations.
  • Your call history and subscription/usage records.
  • Group memberships and groups you created.

What may be retained, and for how long

  • Messages you sent that are still visible in an active conversation may remain accessible to the other participant(s) of that conversation, consistent with how most chat apps handle shared conversation history — deleting your account removes your own profile and access, not the other party's copy of a shared chat.
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that no longer identify you may be retained for basic service analytics.
  • Records we are legally required to keep (e.g., for fraud prevention or legal compliance) are retained only as long as required by law, then deleted.

Partial data deletion

You can delete individual messages you sent at any time from within the app (long-press a message → Delete), without deleting your whole account.

For any questions about this process, contact oalaff@smail.ucas.edu.ps.
